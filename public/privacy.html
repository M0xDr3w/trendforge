Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

1. Overview

TrendForge ("we", "us", or "our") provides a real-time X (Twitter) content analysis and trend visualization tool. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

  • X Data: When authorized, we access public X posts, trends, and related data via the X API on your behalf to analyze conversations, clusters, and narrative shifts.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage information such as which features you interact with to improve the tool.
  • Authentication: We use X OAuth 2.0 to obtain access tokens. These tokens are stored securely and used only to fetch data from X on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the data to:

  • Provide real-time trend analysis, clustering, and content suggestions.
  • Generate insights and "sparks" for content creators.
  • Improve the quality and relevance of the tool.

4. Data Sharing and Storage

We do not sell your data. X data is processed in memory or short-term storage for analysis. We do not permanently store full post content beyond what's needed for the current session unless you explicitly export or log it.

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device (via the xurl tool) and are never transmitted to third parties except to X's API.

5. Third-Party Services

TrendForge uses the official X API. Your use of X data is also subject to X's Privacy Policy and Developer Agreement.

6. Your Choices

You can revoke TrendForge's access to your X account at any time from your X settings (Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions).

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data. However, no system is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the date above.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the contact methods listed on the TrendForge site.
